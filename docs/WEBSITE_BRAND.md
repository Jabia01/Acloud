# Acloud website brand colours

The owner requested a clear blue throughout the website, superseding the earlier teal interpretation of the native accent.

- Primary blue: **#0066CC**. White button labels meet WCAG AA contrast.
- Deep blue: **#123C73**.
- Pale blue: **#E8F2FF**.
- CSS tokens: `--brand` and `--brand-deep` in `apps/web/app/styles.css`.

Apply blue to actions, navigation, banners, illustrations and healthy-state accents. Keep warning/error colours distinct and always accompany statuses with text. Colour never grants protection or implies unverified health. Native app source is unchanged in this website-only update.
